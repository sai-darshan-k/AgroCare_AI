Kissan 4.0: Business Model Timeline

Key Business Model Points

  • Year 1: B2C Focus with C2C Exploration - Deploy IoT poles to 7-8K users generating ₹2.4Cr through hardware sales and subscriptions while researching e-Market feasibility.
  • Year 2: B2C Expansion with C2C Launch - Scale pole sales and subscriptions while launching a basic version of Kissan e-Market with initial commissions from farmer-to-farmer transactions.
  • Year 3: B2C + C2C Integration - Achieve 75K users with 40% hardware adoption, generating ₹31.5Cr from subscriptions and scaling the e-Market with 7.5% commissions for a total of ₹37.5Cr revenue.
  • Evolution Strategy - Begin with farmer tools (B2C) and transition to farmer markets (C2C), creating a comprehensive agricultural ecosystem.
