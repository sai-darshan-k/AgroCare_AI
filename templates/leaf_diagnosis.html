How Kissan 4.0 Works

Our advanced AI-powered tool helps farmers and gardeners identify plant diseases instantly from images. Simply upload a photo of your affected plant or capture one in real-time, and our system will analyze it to detect potential diseases.

After analysis, you'll receive comprehensive information including disease identification, organic treatment options, prevention tips, and seasonal risk factors.

Kissan 4.0 can identify 38 different types of plant diseases across various crops including apple, blueberry, cherry, corn, grape, orange, peach, pepper, potato, raspberry, soybean, squash, strawberry, and tomato.
