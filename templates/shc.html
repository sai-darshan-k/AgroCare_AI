About the Scheme

The Soil Health Card Scheme is a flagship program launched by the Government of India to promote soil testing services and issue soil health cards to all farmers every three years. The scheme aims to provide a comprehensive soil fertility assessment and recommend balanced nutrient management practices for sustainable agriculture.

Key Objectives

Regular Assessment

Issue soil health cards every 3 years to all farmers across India

Laboratory Strengthening

Enhance capacity of Soil Testing Laboratories through modernization and training

Standardized Analysis

Implement uniform soil sampling and testing procedures across states

How to Apply Online

1

2

3

Fill in the required information including organization details, user details, and login credentials
5
Login using your username and password
6

Balanced Nutrition

Higher Productivity

Significant increase in crop productivity through optimal nutrient management
